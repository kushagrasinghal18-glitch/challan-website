2. What we do

  • Check the pending traffic e-challans on the vehicle numbers you give us, free of charge.
  • Tell you which challans we can take up at the next Lok Adalat, which we cannot (with the reason) and the price.
  • After your written approval and payment, prepare the paperwork, register or book the Lok Adalat token where needed, and attend the Lok Adalat on your behalf using an authorisation letter you sign.
  • Share the result and the receipt with you on WhatsApp.

We currently serve Delhi, Noida and Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Gurugram. Challans from other places, challans not yet sent to court, and offences the Lok Adalat does not take up (for example drunk driving or accident cases) may be outside our service. We tell you this before you pay.

3. Price and written approval

  • We send you the list of challans and the amount on WhatsApp. You confirm by replying I APPROVE. That reply is your written approval of the list and the amount.
  • You pay only after that approval, using the payment details we send (UPI or bank transfer to Boxsmith Packaging Private Limited). Never pay anyone who contacts you from another number or asks for an OTP, PIN or password; we never ask for these.

4. Results at the Lok Adalat

The Lok Adalat, not Niptao, decides each case. We cannot promise that a challan will be taken up or the amount it will be settled for, and Lok Adalat dates, courts and slots are set by the authorities and can change. A Lok Adalat settlement is final and binding.

5. Refunds

  • 100% refund promise: if a challan you paid us for is not cleared at the Lok Adalat, we refund the full amount you paid for that challan after the Lok Adalat date.
  • If you cancel before we have registered your challans or booked a token for them, we refund the full amount for those challans.
  • After registration or token booking, a cancellation is not refundable, but the refund promise above still applies.
  • Refunds go back to the account you paid from, normally within 7 working days of the refund being due.

6. What we need from you

  • Clear copies of the documents we ask for (such as RC, driving licence and Aadhaar or PAN) and a signed authorisation letter, in time for the Lok Adalat.
  • If documents are missing, wrong or late, we may not be able to settle the challan on that date. In that case we carry it to the next Lok Adalat or refund it as set out above.
